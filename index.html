How it works

One lap = the project

The track is the whole timeline. With 20 days, the baton moves 20 m a day, and the infield counts down to the submit time.

One leg each

Every teammate owns a leg and gets their own lock screen: their leg outlined, their status in plain words, and today's task for whoever has the baton.

Real takeover zones

A 4×100 zone is 30 m: 20 m before the line, 10 m after. Here that is one day of warm-up for the next runner and half a day of standby for the last one, so no handoff is cold.

The plan (worked out with Gemini)

Use it for your team

Send the team link to your group; everyone opens it, picks their leg and downloads their own screens. The plan lives in the link itself.

Change it every morning (iPhone)

  1. Download All days for my leg, unzip it, and put the folder in iCloud Drive/Shortcuts/Relay.
  2. Shortcuts → Automation → New → Time of Day, 6:00 AM, Daily, Run Immediately.
  3. Actions: Current Date → Format Date (custom yyyy-MM-dd) → Get File Relay/[Formatted Date].png → Set Wallpaper Photo (Lock Screen).

On Android, set the day's PNG by hand, or use an automation app that can change the wallpaper on a schedule.

Plan, daily tasks and the stadium painting: made with Gemini. Takeover-zone rule: World Athletics technical rules for 4×100 m relays (30 m zone, scratch line 20 m in). Type: Barlow and Barlow Condensed (SIL Open Font License). Everything else is drawn in the browser from one SVG generator, relay.js.
